Section 01

Introduction & Purpose

Welcome to Crest (hereinafter referred to as the "Application", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy and General Notice outline the operational guidelines and privacy practices of our platform.

Core Objective

Crest operates exclusively as a Digital Resource Guide. Our primary mission is to aggregate and systematically organize essential web links for professionals engaged in freelancing, micro-jobs, application development, and digital marketing.

Our Purpose

Crest is designed to serve as a centralized hub that enhances digital workflow convenience.

Nature of the Application

Please note that Crest functions strictly as a link aggregation tool. The Application provides direct navigational access to various external platforms.

We do not utilize backend databases, proprietary hosting, or dedicated servers to process user activity.

Furthermore, we hold no ownership or administrative control over the third-party platforms linked within our app.

Important Notice

Crest is not associated with any "quick income" schemes or fraudulent activities.

Section 03

Data Privacy & Security

Zero Data Collection Policy

Safeguarding our users' digital privacy is our paramount concern.

Crest is designed to be fully functional without requiring user registration. You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide any personal identification to utilize our Application.

Privacy First

Crest does not require users to submit personal identification simply to access and use the Application.

System Permissions & Security Standards

Because our application operates as a straightforward navigational tool, we do not request intrusive device permissions such as access to contacts, location, or storage.

We categorically state that we do not track, collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) or user documents.

You can utilize Crest with the assurance that your personal data remains uncompromised.
