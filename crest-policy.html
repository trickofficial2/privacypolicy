1. Introduction & Purpose

Welcome to Crest (hereinafter referred to as the "Application", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy and General Notice outline the operational guidelines and privacy practices of our platform.

Core Objective

Crest operates exclusively as a Digital Resource Guide. Our primary mission is to aggregate and systematically organize essential web links for professionals engaged in freelancing, micro-jobs, application development, and digital marketing. We designed this platform to serve as a centralized hub that enhances digital workflow convenience.

Nature of the Application

Please note that Crest functions strictly as a link aggregation tool. The Application provides direct navigational access to various external platforms. We do not utilize backend databases, proprietary hosting, or dedicated servers to process user activity. Furthermore, we hold no ownership or administrative control over the third-party platforms linked within our app. Crest is not associated with any "quick income" schemes or fraudulent activities.

2. User Experience & Benefits

Streamlining Digital Workflows

A fundamental challenge for digital professionals is the constant need to juggle multiple custom browser tabs when searching for resources. This often leads to performance degradation, increased load times, and a fragmented user experience.

Crest mitigates this issue by consolidating critical digital resources into a single, cohesive interface. This structural integration eliminates the need for repeated browser navigation, significantly reducing user friction and saving valuable time.

Key Advantages

  • Enhanced Time Efficiency: Instant access to critical tools without repetitive web searches.
  • Centralized Interface: A systematically organized dashboard of industry-standard resources.
  • Mobile Optimization: The ability to seamlessly execute tasks directly from a mobile device, eliminating the strict necessity for a desktop environment.

3. Data Privacy & Security

Because our application operates as a straightforward navigational tool, we do not request intrusive device permissions (such as access to contacts, location, or storage). We categorically state that we do not track, collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) or user documents. You can utilize Crest with the absolute assurance that your personal data remains uncompromised.
