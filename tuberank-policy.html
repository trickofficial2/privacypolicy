Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Summary: Welcome to TubeRank Pro! Your privacy is very important to us. This application operates with a privacy-first approach by storing data locally on your device and utilizing official services for YouTube data fetching.

1. Information Collection & Storage

TubeRank Pro does not collect, harvest, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) on external servers.

  • All analysis data, saved titles, tags, and custom preferences remain exclusively stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate external databases to record your personal activity, search queries, or extracted metadata.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep TubeRank Pro completely free and to cover ongoing server maintenance, development, and operational costs, we display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdMob).

  • These ad networks may collect anonymized system logs or device identifiers solely for ad serving and fraud detection.
  • No personal photos, saved tags, or search preferences within this app are ever shared with advertisers.

3. YouTube API Services

This application utilizes the official YouTube Data API v3 to fetch public video metadata, including but not limited to:

  • View counts, likes, and engagement metrics
  • Public video tags and extracted high-ranking keywords
  • Search rankings within YouTube search results

By using TubeRank Pro, you acknowledge and agree to abide by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Local Cache

To provide a smooth performance and fast experience, all user preferences, search history, and saved tags are stored in your phone's SharedPreferences.

  • You maintain full control over this data.
  • You can completely clear all cached data and stored preferences at any time directly from the app Settings or through your phone's App Manager.

5. Security & API Proxies

Our API endpoints are protected using enterprise-grade cloud proxies to guarantee security, stability, and fast response times.

  • All network communications between the application and public endpoints are encrypted via standard HTTPS/SSL protocols.
  • No sensitive device identifiers or personal user details are transmitted through our proxies.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in functionality or legal requirements. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any updates.
