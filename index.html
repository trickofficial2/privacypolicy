Digital Resource Guide: A General Notice & Policy Manual

Dear User,

Core Purpose of the Application

The main objective of creating this app (a "Digital Resource Guide") is to organize important website links used for digital work on a simple platform for your convenience.

Our application is not intended for any third-party activities. Our primary goal is to assist professionals involved in Freelancing, Micro Jobs, App Development, and other similar digital and social media-based work. This app has been specifically designed with user convenience in mind.

Nature of the App

This is a link collection app. The links provided here offer direct access to those websites.

This app does not use any database, hosting, or server.

We do not have ownership or control over any of the links or platforms in this app. This is not a quick money-making or fraudulent app.

Benefits of Using the App

Streamlining the User Experience

A key goal in developing our app is to make the user's searching process easier and faster.

We have observed that users frequently search on Google Chrome for information. When they look for a second item, they have to open a new Custom Tab in their browser. This results in multiple tabs being created, which often causes the browser to take a lot of time to load, presenting a problem for users.

To eliminate this issue, we have integrated various features in one place within our app. This prevents the user from having to repeatedly go to the browser and open new tabs, which saves time and ensures a smoother browsing experience.

Key Benefits Checklist

  • Time Saving: Quick access to resources without repeated searching.
  • Ease of Use: All links are well-organized and available in one place.
  • Mobile Convenience: The advantage of starting work directly from your mobile device.

User Security and Data Protection

Data Security and Account Requirement

Ensuring the security of our users' data is our main objective. There is no need to create an account to use our app.

The app will not ask for any special permissions from the user. Since no account is required, we do not store or process any personal data of the users.

Security Standard

We do not steal any of your personal information or documents. Generally, our app does not require any special permissions or the creation of an account to be used. We are committed to maintaining the security of user information.
